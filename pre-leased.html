Own the building. Keep the rent.

Pre-leased property is commercial space already rented to a tenant. You buy it with the lease in place, so rental income starts from the day of registration.

How it works

An office, a tenant and a signed lease, bought together.

A bank, a clinic chain or a technology firm signs a multi-year lease with the owner. The lease fixes a lock-in period, a security deposit and rent escalations, usually every three years.

When you buy that space, the lease transfers to you. You become the landlord, the tenant keeps paying, and you hold an appreciating asset with a predictable cash flow.

Questions investors ask

Pre-leased, answered.

What is the minimum ticket size?

In East Pune, pre-leased shops and small offices start around ₹1.5–2 Cr. Grade-A office floors leased to large companies usually start from ₹5 Cr.

What happens when the lock-in ends?

The tenant can renew, renegotiate or exit with notice. We assess the likelihood of renewal before you buy, based on the tenant's fit-out spend and how important the location is to them.

Can I take a loan against a pre-leased property?

Yes. Banks offer lease rental discounting, where the loan is serviced by the rent itself. We coordinate with lenders as part of the purchase.

Is the rent taxable?

Rent is taxed as income from house property, after a standard deduction. Corporate tenants deduct TDS before paying rent. We recommend reviewing your position with your tax advisor.

Can NRIs invest in pre-leased property?

Yes, NRIs can buy commercial property in India. We help with documentation and coordinate with your advisors on power of attorney and rent repatriation.
