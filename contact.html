Before you call

Common questions

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Kharadi and work across Pune, from Kalyani Nagar, Viman Nagar, Wagholi and Hadapsar in the east to Baner, Wakad, Hinjewadi and PCMC in the west.

Can you arrange site visits for several projects on one day?

Yes. Book an appointment with the projects you're interested in and we'll plan the visits together.

Do you help with home loans and interiors?

Yes. We connect you with suitable home loan options and help with documentation, and our interior design service can take your new home from bare shell to move-in ready.

Can you help if I live outside India?

Yes. We can run video walkthroughs and help with documentation for NRI buyers.
